📊 1. General Statistics Concepts

Before diving into basketball, let’s understand the core statistical tools using a simple dataset.

Sample Dataset

Scores: [5, 7, 8, 10, 10, 12, 15]

Mean (Average)

Sum ÷ Count

(5+7+8+10+10+12+15) ÷ 7 = 9.57
Median

Middle value in sorted list

[5,7,8,10,10,12,15] → Median = 10
Mode

Most frequent value

10 appears twice → Mode = 10
Range

Max – Min

15 – 5 = 10

Example Calculation

Player A’s Efficiency (EFF) and Plus/Minus (+/-):

  • EFF: (PTS + REB + AST + STL + BLK) – ((FGA – FGM) + (FTA – FTM) + TO) = (24+8+5+2+1) – (9+1+3) = 40 – 6 = +34
  • +/-: Team scored 58 points and allowed 43 while Player A was on court → +15
